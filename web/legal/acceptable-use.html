Acceptable Use

Effective 14 October 2026

This policy sets out what you may not do with LexBharat. It forms part of the Terms of Service.

  • Copy the library in bulk, scrape it, or use the Service to build a competing database or to train a model.
  • Try to get around limits, sign-in, rate controls or other protections, or probe, overload or attack the Service.
  • Use someone else's account, create accounts to avoid limits, or give false details when signing up.
