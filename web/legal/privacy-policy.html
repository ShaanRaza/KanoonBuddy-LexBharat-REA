Privacy Policy

Effective 14 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data LexBharat collects, why, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. It is written to meet the requirements of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the "DPDP Act").

Who is responsible for your data

LexBharat
Email: support@lexbharat.ai

Grievance Officer: support@lexbharat.ai

What we collect, and why

  • Account details: your name and email address, and a one-way scrambled form of your password (we cannot read your password). We use them to create and secure your account and to email you account messages such as confirmation and password-reset links.
  • Your consent record: that you accepted the Terms and this Policy, which version, and when.
  • What you ask and save: the questions you ask KanoonBuddy, the answers, and documents you save in the editor. We use them to give you the Service, show you your history and let you return to your work.
  • Use counts: how many questions you have asked today, to apply the daily limit.
  • Technical and security data: your IP address and the pages and requests made to our servers. These help us keep the Service secure and stop abuse. They are held in server logs for up to 30 days.
  • On your device: a sign-in token and small settings are kept in your browser's storage so you stay signed in. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies and do not run third-party trackers.

We do not ask for, and you should not enter, sensitive identifiers such as Aadhaar, PAN or bank details.

Your consent

We process your data on the basis of the consent you give when you create an account, and for the limited purposes the DPDP Act allows without consent, such as meeting legal obligations. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account. Withdrawing means we can no longer provide the Service to you.

How the AI part works

When you ask a question, we search our library of statutes and judgments for relevant passages and send your question and those passages to a language model, which writes the answer. The model runs on infrastructure we control. We do not use your questions or documents to train a model, and we do not sell them.

Who else sees your data

  • Service providers that help us run the Service, such as our hosting provider and our email-delivery provider (which sees your email address and the message). They may use data only to provide their service to us.
  • Bot protection, if switched on at sign-up, is provided by Cloudflare Turnstile, which receives your IP address and browser details to tell people from bots.
  • Authorities, where the law requires us to disclose data.

We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

  • Chats: deleted automatically after 180 days. You can delete them earlier.
  • Saved documents and account details: kept until you delete them or your account.
  • Deleted accounts: when you ask us to delete your account we keep it for 30 days so you can change your mind, then permanently delete the account, your chats and your documents. Backups, if any, are overwritten on a regular cycle.
  • Server logs: up to 30 days.
  • We may keep limited records for longer where the law requires it.

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can:

  • Get a copy of your data: Account settings, then Privacy & data, then Export as JSON.
  • Correct or update it: change details in Account settings, or write to us.
  • Have it erased: Account settings, then Close this account, then Delete my account (see Delete your data).
  • Complain: write to our Grievance Officer above. We will acknowledge your complaint and aim to resolve it within 30 days. If you are not satisfied you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
  • Nominate another person to exercise these rights for you if you die or become incapacitated.

How we protect it

Passwords are stored only as salted one-way hashes. Sign-in tokens expire and can be revoked, and logging out or changing your password ends other sessions. We limit sign-in attempts and the rate of requests, and we only collect data we need. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires. See also Security.

Children

The Service is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you think a child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

Where data is processed

Your data is stored and processed on servers chosen by the operator; we will name the region here. If this changes we will update this policy.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you and, where required, ask for your consent again.
