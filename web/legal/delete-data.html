Delete your account

  1. Sign in and open Account settings.
  2. Under Close this account, choose Delete my account.

We keep the account for 30 days in case you change your mind, and you can cancel it from the same page (choose Keep my account) during that time. After that your account, your chats and your saved documents are permanently deleted.

Export first

In Account settings, open Privacy & data and choose Export as JSON to download a copy of your information.

Can't sign in?

Write to support@lexbharat.ai from the email address on the account and we will help. We may ask you to confirm it is you before we delete anything.
