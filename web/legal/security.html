Security

Effective 14 October 2026

This page says plainly what we do to protect your account and data, and what we do not claim.

What we do

  • Passwords are never stored as text. They are stored only as salted one-way hashes (scrypt).
  • Sign-in uses signed tokens that expire. Logging out, changing your password or disabling an account ends the sessions.
  • Sign-in attempts are limited, and repeated failures lock the account for a while. Sign-up and password-reset emails are rate limited and their links work once and expire.
  • Your chats and saved documents are visible only to your account.
  • Connections are encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Chats are deleted automatically after 180 days, and you can export or delete your data yourself.
  • We do not run advertising or analytics trackers on the site.

What we do not claim

We do not hold third-party security certifications, and we have not yet had an independent security audit. No online service is perfectly secure.

Report a problem

If you find a security issue, please write to support@lexbharat.ai with the details. Give us reasonable time to fix it before you share it publicly. Please do not access other people's data while testing.
