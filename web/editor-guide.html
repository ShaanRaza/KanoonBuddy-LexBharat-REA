Editor guide

A plain document editor with legal templates, file import and an assistant beside it. Here is what each part does, and what it does not do yet.

Open the editor

What you can do

Start from a template

Click Templates. Pick a standard Indian document (affidavits, legal notices, a rent agreement, an NDA, a power of attorney), fill in the form, and insert it. Blank fields stay as [BRACKETS] for you to complete. The notices have an optional box for the advocate sending them.

Import a file

Click Import to bring in a PDF, Word (.docx), text or RTF file, or a scanned image or PDF (read with OCR, up to 30 pages). Old .doc files must be saved as .docx first.

Ask KanoonBuddy

Open the assistant to ask legal questions while you write. Under each reply, Insert into document puts it at your cursor and Copy copies it. Drafts written by the assistant can be wrong, so always read and edit them.

Check document

Click Check to find blanks you have not filled in, check every section and case you cite against the library, and see whether any cited law is repealed.

Save and come back

Click Save. Your documents are kept in your account, under My Documents (the panel icon at the top left). The first line becomes the title.

Cmd/Ctrl+ZUndo (add Shift to redo)
Cmd/Ctrl+FFind in the document

Not available yet

Export Not yet

Download as Word or PDF is not built. For now, copy your text into Word.

Court-specific formatting and tracked changes Not yet

The editor does not detect the court or apply court margins, and the assistant does not rewrite selected text or track changes. It only replies in the chat.

LexBharat is a research aid, not legal advice. Check every section, citation and draft against the official source.
