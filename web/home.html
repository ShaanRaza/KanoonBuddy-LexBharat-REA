Know the limits

  • KanoonBuddy is an AI model and can be wrong. Check every section and citation against the official text before you rely on it. It is a research aid, not legal advice.
  • Not yet included: the Code of Criminal Procedure 1973, the Code of Civil Procedure 1908 and the Constitution as statute text. District courts, live court status and cause lists are not covered.
  • Judgment text comes from a public dataset and can contain scanning errors. Excerpts are shown as excerpts, not the whole judgment.
  • Free during early access, with a daily limit on questions. Paid plans are not available yet.
