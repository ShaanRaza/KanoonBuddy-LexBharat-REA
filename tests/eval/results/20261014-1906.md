### S13 (statute): What is the longest period a magistrate can authorise detention in police custody under BNSS 187?
- failed checks: **facts**
- retrieved: BNSS § 187
- answer: Under [BNSS § 187(3)], the longest period a magistrate can authorise detention in police custody is:  - Ninety days for offences punishable with death, imprisonment for life, or imprisonment for a term of ten years or more. - Sixty days for other offences.  These periods can be extended further only if adequate grounds exist and the accused is produced before the Magistrate as required.
